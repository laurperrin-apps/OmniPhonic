OmniPhonic support

Have a question, a playback issue or a suggestion? Start with these checks, or contact us directly.

A radio station will not play

  • Check your Internet connection and try another station.
  • Some streams change their URLs or are restricted in certain countries.
  • If the issue persists, tell us the station name and, if possible, its streaming URL.

A podcast episode will not load

  • Check whether the episode is available on the publisher’s website.
  • Try a different network and restart the app.
  • For downloaded episodes, check the available storage on your iPhone.

An audiobook is missing or will not play

  • Results depend on the LibriVox, AudioCité and Internet Archive catalogs.
  • Try searching by book title or author, and perhaps in another language.
  • For a personal file, check that iOS supports its audio format.

Sync and offline listening

  • iCloud synchronization requires an Apple Account and compatible iCloud settings.
  • Large downloaded audio files remain on the device and are not uploaded by the app’s iCloud synchronization.
  • Download content before your trip if you intend to listen offline.

CarPlay

  • Check that your iPhone is connected to a CarPlay-compatible vehicle.
  • Available screens and controls can vary based on your vehicle and Apple restrictions.
  • For safe driving, certain search and management tasks must be performed on the iPhone while parked.

Contact the developer

Email the address below with your iPhone model, iOS version, OmniPhonic version, relevant content and a description of the issue. Never send your password.

OmniPhonic@icloud.com

OmniPhonic does not operate third-party streams, catalog services or hosting servers. Interruptions affecting those services can disrupt listening.
