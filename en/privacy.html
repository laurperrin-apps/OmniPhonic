Privacy policy

Last updated : November 9, 2026

This page describes how OmniPhonic, an iOS radio, podcast and audiobook app developed by Laurent Perrin, handles information.

1. Data and app operation

You can browse radio, podcast and audiobook catalogs without creating an OmniPhonic account.

Favorites, followed podcasts, history, playback positions, preferences and download-related information may be stored on your device.

When iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, some of this data may be stored and synchronized using Apple services associated with your account.

2. Downloads and personal files

Downloaded podcast episodes and audiobooks are stored locally on your device.

Personal audio files you import are used for playback and organization within the app.

These large audio files are not part of the app’s own iCloud synchronization.

3. External services

Depending on the features used, the app connects to third-party sources such as Radio Browser, Apple/iTunes podcast search services, RSS feeds, LibriVox, AudioCité and Internet Archive.

These services may receive technical information required to process requests, such as your IP address and search terms, and they have their own privacy policies.

When streaming audio, your device connects to the servers hosting the selected content.

4. Advertising, tracking and analytics

In the app version reviewed for this update, we did not identify an OmniPhonic-specific user account, ad network or advertising SDK.

Apple and third-party services may nonetheless process technical or diagnostic information according to their own settings and policies.

5. Retention and your controls

You can manage favorites and remove downloads and content using the relevant app features.

Deleting the app generally removes local app data, subject to iOS backup behavior. Data synchronized with iCloud may remain depending on your Apple Account settings.

6. Your rights and contact

For questions about your personal information or to exercise applicable data protection rights, contact the developer at the address below.

This policy may be updated when app features or third-party services change.
